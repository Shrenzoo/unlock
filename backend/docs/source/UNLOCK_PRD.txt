1 Document Information and Executive Summary
Decision. Proceed with a gated hackathon prototype of an AI assisted repairability redesign and verification engine. Support a small STEP assembly plus a required engineering manifest. Implement one parametric bracket family with two bounded interventions. Preserve the complete workflow: Import, Select, Analyze, Explain, Redesign, Verify, Compare, Export.
Core promise. An engineer selects a difficult to replace component. UNLOCK proposes a constrained design edit, constructs the changed geometry, and independently checks the resulting removal and reverse insertion paths within its supported model.
The central engineering deliverable is a versioned evidence package linking the input assembly, declared constraints, baseline plan, candidate parameters, revised solid geometry, and verification results. A proposal is never a verified result. A visible animation is never a collision certificate. Geometric feasibility is never product safety or release approval.
Recommended MVP. One appliance inspired service module, 5 to 12 rigid component occurrences including simplified fasteners, axis aligned translation only, explicit screwdriver approach volumes, and server owned parametric bracket edits. The bracket must already be independently fastened; the MVP does not split arbitrary welded or integral structures. The target, base, mounting interfaces, fixed parts, and declared keep out regions remain protected.
Research conclusion. General disassembly planning, tool access analysis, and redesign for selected part access have substantial prior art. The defensible product thesis is a usable, auditable workflow for early service design reviews, with a tightly defined compatibility contract and editable changes. Neither research novelty nor patent freedom is established. See Sections 5 and 6. [S07-S16]
Event status. The organizer page confirms the event dates, Indie and Startup tracks, themes, and use of Meta models. The supplied October 26 team guide reports a public repository, accessible working demo, three minute video, 1 to 4 members, and scoring weights of 30/25/20/25. Those details retain their supplied source status; the signed rules and every participant's eligibility require confirmation. The waiver was inaccessible through the research browser. Plan to submit November 4 at 8:00 p.m. America/Chicago, matching 9:00 p.m. Eastern; verify the portal because the landing page uses the label EST across a daylight saving transition. [S01-S03]
Evidence vocabulary. FACT means directly documented by an identified primary source. RESEARCH means an author's reported result, not reproduced here. INFERENCE means an engineering interpretation. HYPOTHESIS means an untested product or commercial assumption. REQUIREMENT means a proposed implementation contract. EXAMPLE means illustrative behavior, never measured performance. All numeric implementation thresholds in this document are provisional requirements unless explicitly attributed. No UNLOCK software benchmarks, user interviews, or successful CAD edits were performed for this PRD.
Change control. Developer A owns workflow and integration; Developer B owns geometry, planning, and the model adapter. Both sign off changes to units, tolerance policy, supported operations, templates, metrics, and evidence wording. Such changes increment the policy version and invalidate affected results.
2 Product Vision and Positioning
One sentence. UNLOCK helps product engineers redesign supported assemblies so a selected service component can be replaced through a checked geometric path.
Elevator pitch. Replacing a $5 part should not require destroying a $500 product. UNLOCK exposes the design choices that obstruct replacement, applies a small supported design change, and shows the resulting access path alongside the checks that support it. The dollar amounts are a rhetorical example, not a measured customer case.
Mission. Make access to service components an explicit design decision before tooling and manufacturing commitments.
Value proposition. For a mechanical engineer reviewing a small electromechanical module, UNLOCK brings the selected repair target, candidate bracket edit, preserved constraints, and comparable access evidence into one review. The hoped for advantage is less manual coordination between inspection, geometry editing, motion checks, and reporting; this advantage needs direct comparison with existing CAD workflows.
Three sentence technical pitch. UNLOCK combines structured assembly metadata with bounded graph search and CAD based geometry checks. Muse Spark Open selects and parameterizes interventions from an approved library using roles, constraints, and tool feedback. A separate verifier evaluates the revised solids and repair sequence and produces a reproducible report.
Position A, an AI powered CAD repairability analyzer, emphasizes an already populated inspection category. Position B, an AI assisted repairability redesign and verification engine, names the edit and recheck loop and is the recommended position. Use the qualifier supported assemblies wherever broad compatibility might otherwise be inferred. Avoid claims of first, autonomous engineering, optimal repair, or universally repairable products.
The longer term vision is a design review layer integrated with native CAD and engineering change control. Broader repairability assessment, cables, tolerances, forces, and manufacturing analysis are separate research and product programs.
3 Problem Statement and Supporting Research
The first problem is deciding whether a high priority component is accessible for replacement without unnecessary disturbance of surrounding parts. Geometry alone is insufficient: connectors, fastener engagement, adhesives, service information, spare parts, and product operating constraints can determine practical repairability.
iFixit's methodology traces paths to critical components and includes tools, actions, and reassembly, alongside parts availability and documentation. This supports target selection and explicit operation counts, while showing why a geometry only result cannot be a complete repairability score. [S04]
The JRC repair scoring framework identifies priority parts and product specific assessment criteria and calls for transparent background information. EN 45554:2020 supplies general assessment methods for repair, reuse, and upgrade of energy related products; the public BSI summary does not provide the full standard. UNLOCK should borrow the discipline of explicit scope and evidence, and must not claim certification or a standardized score. [S05-S06]
Engineering distinctions. Design for Assembly favors repeatable, economical construction. Design for Disassembly addresses separation for purposes including service and recovery. Design for Repair adds the target failure, replacement part, reconnection, and restoration of function. A design that is easy to assemble may bury a fastener behind later installed parts; a destructive recycling route may be unsuitable for repair.
Supported barriers are a released component obstructed by another solid, an inadequate extraction corridor, or a declared fastener whose approach volume is obstructed. Adhesives, clips requiring deformation, cable routing, corrosion, press fits, and electrical isolation can be decisive but are excluded from the MVP. A service opening or reduced bracket web may weaken a load path, ingress protection, or electromagnetic shielding. Preserving hole centers does not preserve those properties.
The European Commission describes repair policy and product specific ecodesign measures as incentives for product life extension. Its repair of goods directive page gives July 31 2026 as the date for national transposition and application. This is a policy signal, not a universal repair obligation for every small appliance or proof of compliance by UNLOCK. Product category and national applicability need specialist review. [S17]
Commercial evidence boundary. Public assessment methods and engineering tools establish that access and serviceability are real engineering concerns. They do not establish how frequently a particular design team encounters the problem or whether that team will pay for this workflow. No waste reduction, warranty saving, repair time reduction, or market size is claimed.
4 Target Users and Jobs to Be Done
Primary user hypothesis. A mechanical design engineer at a small appliance or electromechanical product company, reviewing a new low complexity service module before design freeze. The engineer can supply STEP data, identify an intended replaceable component, and declare mounting and keep out requirements. This user controls the design and can act on a proposed edit; a repair shop usually cannot change products already manufactured.
Existing workflow. Open the native CAD assembly, isolate the service component, inspect attachments, move or suppress surrounding parts, try extraction, modify geometry, rerun clearance checks, and document a design review. Existing CAD inspection and extraction tools support important pieces of this process. UNLOCK's adoption question is whether it reduces the manual effort to carry a selected service target through a candidate edit and an evidence backed comparison. [S07-S10]
The decision is whether to revise a supported bracket before release, leave the original design, or request further engineering analysis. The expected occasion is a serviceability review or engineering change, not continuous daily use. Frequency, cost of current work, and the ability to share geometry remain unvalidated.

Sequence and measurable outcomes. AssembleX and SBDP explicitly search sequences; NX documents extraction path planning, which is not proof of a global disassembly sequence search. The fastener study reports analytic changes to planning objectives after removing candidate fasteners. Its metrics and AssembleX's simulated time proxy are not measured human repair times. The patent describes optimization goals without establishing achieved commercial performance. Commercial inspection documentation establishes capabilities, not independent performance benchmarks. [S10-S16]
Strategic implication. Match basic selection, hierarchy, clearance display, and explainable findings. Differentiate on the evidence package joining one repair target, a constrained editable patch, original and revised sequence checks, protected constraints, and rejection of invalid edits. Do not position an exploded view, accessibility heatmap, or AI explanation alone as the breakthrough.
Public vendor pages do not settle real workflow quality, pricing, or all available modules. No systematic customer review study or independent tool trial supports a claim that engineers dislike the incumbents. Test the exact workflow against an experienced user's installed CAD before committing to a standalone commercial product. Integration may be a stronger distribution model than CAD replacement.
6 Prior Art Novelty and Differentiation
AssembleX. The repository accepts part meshes and produces disassembly paths, rendered steps, manuals, tool decisions, and feedback. Its active backend considers physical and tool constraints. The associated October 7 2026 paper reports evaluation on 136 assemblies and a robot time proxy. This establishes a strong adjacent end to end planning baseline; its reviewed materials do not demonstrate the proposed editable bracket repair workflow. Source availability and author evaluation are not independent replication. Its MIT wrapper does not override upstream backend licenses. [S12-S13]
Fastener reduction. The May 20 2026 paper computes influence on a CAD derived Contact Connection Constraint graph, ranks fasteners, and analytically evaluates removal candidates. Its seven appliance study is stronger prior art than a mere obstruction visualizer. The discussion explicitly excludes material strength and robot dynamics and describes generative geometry changes as future work. Treat its geometric stability indicators as proxies, not structural certification. [S15]
Patent review. Wayne State University's US20250390643A1 was published December 25 2025 with priority June 20 2024; the inspected record labels it pending. Claim 17 covers a target part and MDDN, accessibility optimized network generation, CAD conversion and presentation. Claims 18 to 20 add feedback and training provisions; claims 1 and 9 concern model training. Description embodiments also address collisions and degradation. [S16]
The selected component redesign concept has direct overlap. UNLOCK's narrower implementation is not an established legal distinction. Pending claims, description embodiments and demonstrated software are different evidence. Obtain a current file history and professional claim analysis before commercialization; no infringement, validity, patentability or freedom conclusion is made. [S16]
Other planning and design optimization. SBDP uses stored motion states, physics simulation, and directional blocking information to improve disassembly search; reimplementing general simulation is unnecessary for this prototype. IDfRA reports iterative planning, execution assessment, and replanning, so the general propose and verify loop is also established. Neither provides a basis to label this broad architecture new. [S14, S23]
Credible contribution to demonstrate. A selected component service objective drives a supported geometry patch; the same protected constraints and verification policy apply before and after; a separate checker produces an inspectable path and evidence; a user can alter a supported constraint or target and see the candidate change or fail. Test this integration on meaningful fixtures. Research publication novelty requires additional work beyond the hackathon product claim.

A successful improvement changes no protected constraints, passes all mandatory checks, and either changes a conclusively infeasible baseline to feasible or improves the feasible baseline's primary objective. For feasible baselines use the lexicographic tuple: unrelated parts, fasteners, tool changes, operations. The first differing value must decrease; no other listed count may increase. Clearance must meet the original threshold, and at most one bracket occurrence may be modified. This restrictive rule prevents arbitrary tradeoffs from looking like improvements.
Do not compute percentage reductions from missing or infeasible baseline counts. Show infeasible to feasible as a status transition. Compare plans under identical units, actions, constraints, staging region, tolerance policy, hardware budget and target. If either search is incomplete, label plan comparisons best found; never claim a globally optimal repair route.

10 Detailed Feature Specifications
10.1 Import and semantic completion
Accept a ZIP containing assembly.step, manifest.json, and optional attribution.txt. A one STEP plus manifest two file upload is equivalent. Reject meshes as analysis input. Limit to 25 MB compressed, 100 MB expanded, 5 to 12 leaf occurrences, 30,000 faces total, and 200,000 rendered triangles. These starting limits protect a two developer CPU prototype and must be calibrated on fixture imports. Refuse external STEP references, encrypted ZIPs, executables, symlinks and path traversal.
Import hierarchy and occurrences through XDE; preserve source transforms and separate repeated occurrences. Display import status independently from semantic readiness. Missing connections, axis, tool, or contact declarations produce a field specific readiness checklist. A JSON editor or form may complete metadata; no inference may silently become an attachment fact. A single fused solid cannot be treated as a complete assembly.
10.2 Viewer and target
Provide orbit, zoom, fit, component list, hide, isolate and transparency. Canvas selection and tree selection resolve to the same stable occurrence ID. Show a target chip, direction arrows, fixed part lock labels, and a return to assembled state control. Hiding is a display action and does not remove a part from collision checks. Target selection requires explicit replaceable status, role, rigid replacement equivalence, connections and staging direction.
10.3 Baseline and explanation
The baseline returns one feasible checked sequence, a conclusive exhaustion under supported rules, or an incomplete result. Each rejected move records the state, proposed operation, obstacle occurrence, geometry witness if available, and policy version. A blocked target does not prove redesign is required: search first considers removable obstructions. Explain the shortest found route and its cost, or why the current search has no supported solution. Do not label a bracket unnecessary based solely on collision geometry.
10.4 Bracket family and interventions
The first supported bracket is a server reconstructed rectangular bridge bracket: two protected feet with unchanged hole axes and mounting pads, and an editable web region. Its construction parameters and expected solid must match the uploaded bracket within tolerance before editing. A positive volume difference, protected face mismatch, or ambiguous orientation makes it analysis only. The model never receives permission to execute arbitrary CAD code.
Constructor v1 geometry. The manifest binds the occurrence to a server owned family version and a local frame. Local Z is the mounting normal; foot bottoms lie at Z zero and foot centers at X plus or minus half the declared span. Required original parameters are span, foot width, foot depth, foot thickness, hole diameter, post width, post depth, web depth, web thickness and web underside height, all in millimeters. Each foot has a centered Z axis through hole. Rectangular posts join the feet to the web. For T01, center x and y, width and depth define a rectangular opening in the web's local XY plane, cut through the full web thickness along Z; reject any intersection with protected foot, hole or post regions. For T02, translate only the web along local Y and regenerate rectangular top transition links between the original posts and the offset web. Every accepted construction must remain one connected valid solid. All original dimensions, allowed deltas and protected signatures are part of the binding hash. This explicit primitive family enables editable output without inferring a feature tree from arbitrary STEP geometry.

Both interventions are branches of one family and are implemented from trusted constructors. T01 is the first to build. T02 is part of the intended model selection test; if time forces its removal, retain T01, disclose the narrower candidate choice, and avoid claiming broad intervention reasoning. Neither template establishes strength or manufacturing feasibility. The engineer supplies allowed design regions and dimensional minima; those minima are geometric inputs, not a safety model.
10.5 Verification and comparison
Verify the revised assembled geometry, protected interfaces, staging corridor, tool access, disassembly and reverse insertion. Store a report per constraint and action. A candidate with an unresolved mandatory condition is ineligible for a verified label. Compare the same selected target and policy; show unchanged declared limits, changed geometry, a metrics table and outstanding physical questions. Display separate labels for geometry checks passed, engineer accepted for export, and engineering release not assessed.
10.6 Export
Export a ZIP with original STEP, revised STEP, changed bracket STEP, revised manifest, template parameters JSON, metrics JSON, action paths JSON, verification JSON, and human readable summary. Include the trusted bracket constructor version or source reference and a parameter file so the supported change can be regenerated. STEP provides editable solid geometry; it does not preserve the originating CAD feature history or native mates. GLB is a preview only. Reimport the exported assembly and rerun identity, transform, volume, and path checks before releasing it. If assembly export fails, report failure and offer a clearly labeled changed part STEP plus manifest and report; do not mark full assembly export complete.
11 Functional Requirements
All FR records are proposed P0 requirements. The user story is the intended user need; the following input, output, acceptance and failure statements define implementation. Backlog IDs map to these records.
FR001 Supported package import
Story. As an engineer I can load a supported module without losing individual parts. Depends on package validator and XDE adapter. Input ZIP or STEP plus manifest. Output immutable assembly revision and component inventory. Acceptance Given a valid fixture with repeated occurrences, when imported, then names, hierarchy, placements and separate occurrence IDs match the fixture oracle. Errors invalid solids, size limit, external reference or missing file produce an import error and no analysis ready revision.
FR002 Semantic readiness
Story. I can see what engineering information is missing. Depends on FR001 and manifest schema. Input imported inventory and metadata. Output per field readiness report. Acceptance Given a missing fastener axis or unresolved component ID, when validation runs, then analysis is blocked with the exact field and expected type. Errors cycles, unsupported connections, units mismatch and ambiguous occurrence mapping require correction; the model cannot auto approve them.
FR003 Assembly inspection
Story. I can understand and select the assembly. Depends on FR001 and mesh mapping. Input GLB plus occurrence metadata. Output interactive viewer and tree. Acceptance Given a hidden bracket, when I analyze, then it still participates in all checks. Selecting a canvas occurrence selects the same tree entry. Errors WebGL or asset failure offers the component list, evidence and retry; analysis readiness is independent of rendering success.
FR004 Repair target selection
Story. I can define which component must be replaceable. Depends on FR002 and FR003. Input occurrence ID and replacement contract. Output target scoped run specification. Acceptance Given a protected base selected as target, when analysis is requested, then selection is refused with a reason. Valid targets show connections, directions and replacement equivalence. Errors unknown or ambiguous ID cannot start a run.
FR005 Fastener operation validation
Story. The plan respects how the parts are retained. Depends on FR002 and geometry engine. Input screw metadata, tool volume and current state. Output removable action or rejection evidence. Acceptance Given an approach blocked by a cover, when the screw is evaluated, then no remove action is admitted until the cover is absent or an independently checked access edit succeeds. Errors unknown engagement or tool geometry yields unknown, never pass.
FR006 Translation operation validation
Story. The plan checks the whole movement. Depends on FR002 and geometry engine. Input released occurrence, permitted direction and travel. Output path certificate or collision or unknown. Acceptance Given a thin obstruction between clear endpoints, when the move is tested, then a verified route is refused. Errors conservative proxy overlap without a CAD witness is unknown; numerical failure cannot mean no collision.
FR007 Baseline sequence search
Story. I can find access without assuming a redesign is necessary. Depends on FR005 and FR006. Input assembly revision, target and policy. Output sequence or scoped failure with budgets. Acceptance Given two ways to remove a blocker, when bounded deterministic search completes, then its returned sequence reaches the target goal and replays through the validator. Errors timeout, state limit or unresolved edges yields incomplete status, not impossibility.
FR008 Obstruction evidence
Story. I can inspect why access failed. Depends on FR007. Input rejected transitions. Output blocker records with state, action and witness. Acceptance Given a collision rejection, when I select it, then the responsible occurrence and failed movement pose are displayed from stored evidence. Errors an approximate proxy warning has a different label from a confirmed CAD intersection.
FR009 Model tool loop
Story. AI can investigate a supported repair redesign. Depends on FR008 and model adapter. Input roles, constraints, baseline evidence and tool catalog. Output validated tool events and proposals. Acceptance Given two supported constraint variants, when the model is run, then the logged request, result and proposal cite matching revision and evidence IDs. Errors malformed arguments, invented tools or unavailable model are rejected; deterministic analysis remains viewable.
FR010 Candidate schema and bounds
Story. A proposal stays within the declared edit scope. Depends on FR009 and template registry. Input template ID, bracket ID, parameters and evidence IDs. Output admitted candidate or typed rejection. Acceptance Given an out of range relief width, when proposed, then construction never starts and the violation returns to the model. Errors extra properties, changed protected IDs or unsupported template never become executable instructions.
FR011 Real geometry modification
Story. I can inspect the actual proposed design. Depends on FR010 and trusted constructors. Input admitted parameters and original revision. Output revised BRep, STEP and patch manifest. Acceptance Given T01, when constructed, then the bracket's geometric difference is nonempty, inside the allowed region, and all other occurrence solids and transforms remain unchanged. Errors failed Boolean or invalid result aborts that candidate without touching the original.
FR012 Independent constraint checks
Story. AI cannot approve its own design. Depends on FR011 and verifier. Input candidate revision and original constraints. Output per constraint pass, fail or unknown records. Acceptance Given a modified mounting pad, when checked, then the candidate is rejected even if its extraction path is clear. Errors missing mandatory check, tolerance ambiguity or stale constraint hash prevents a verified status.
FR013 Revised plan and reinsertion
Story. The change improves a checked replacement route. Depends on FR012 and FR007. Input candidate and original planning policy. Output revised route and reverse path checks. Acceptance Given a candidate with valid assembled clearance but obstructed screw reinsertion, when checked, then it is ineligible. Errors unresolved reverse operations appear in the report rather than being inferred from animation.
FR014 Comparable metrics
Story. I can assess the actual difference. Depends on FR007 and FR013. Input matching original and revised reports. Output defined metrics and improvement decision. Acceptance Given an infeasible baseline, when compared with a feasible candidate, then the display shows a status transition and no invented percentage or baseline operation count. Errors different targets, constraints or policies prevent comparison.
FR015 Evidence based path playback
Story. The visual route corresponds to the check. Depends on FR003 and FR013. Input stored path segments and transforms. Output play, pause, step and scrub controls. Acceptance Given reduced motion preference, when a path is shown, then no movement autoplays and each pose remains inspectable. 13 CAD Input and Data Requirements
Compatibility contract v1. Self contained STEP AP214 or AP242 with separate closed manifold solids and a JSON manifest. Accept 5 to 12 leaf occurrences, including fasteners, within a 500 mm axis aligned envelope. Each moving occurrence is rigid. Template editable brackets must match the known family. Others are immutable. Native CAD files, STL, OBJ, single fused bodies, unsupported contact types and flexible parts are analysis unsupported.
XDE carries useful assembly and attribute information; it does not authorize assumptions about operational fasteners or service actions. Some STEP data can carry richer product information, but the MVP does not consume arbitrary mate, thread, cable or joint semantics. The manifest remains the explicit contract. [S19-S21]
Frames. Millimeters, right handed world frame, Z up, homogeneous 4 by 4 transforms expressed as row major arrays applied to column vectors. STEP placement is authoritative; manifest references source occurrence paths and may repeat placement only for validation. Do not apply a transform twice. Reject scale, shear, reflection or duplicate ambiguous occurrence IDs. Geometry may be rotated at import, but supported extraction directions must align with world positive or negative X, Y or Z.
Identity. Derive source occurrence path and per import stable ID; never rely only on display names. Repeated part definitions get distinct occurrence IDs and shared geometry definition hashes. Bracket edits are occurrence local. Rebuilding cannot modify every instance of a shared definition accidentally. Face IDs from imported STEP are not stable after arbitrary edits; protected interfaces use constructor named features and geometric signatures, not only topology indices.
Required semantic records. 14 Disassembly Planning Specification
State. A set of removed occurrence IDs, remaining connection states derived from that set, and the last tool ID used for tool operations. All remaining occurrences stay at original placements. No partially displaced state, subassembly carrying, gravity simulation, or tool left inside an assembly is supported. The base is grounded by declaration; the assumed workholding is displayed.
Primitive actions. REMOVE_FASTENER requires an annotated reusable screw, reachable tool approach and removal volume, and a released connection transition. Tool rotation and thread engagement are abstracted into one declared action; force and torque are not simulated. TRANSLATE_PART requires all retainers removed, a permitted axis, and a continuously checked route to an external staging region. The action removes the whole occurrence from future obstacle sets. The target goal is reached only after target translation to staging.
Connections. A retained_by relation prevents moving the host until every listed fastener is removed. A planar_seat relation specifies start contact that can separate only in an allowed outward normal direction. No welded, adhesive, snap, press fit, wire or hose relation is executable. If one is present in the target dependency closure, readiness fails. Do not simply delete the unsupported connection.
Search choice. Deterministic lexicographic uniform cost search with memoization, using counts from Section 7. The state includes last tool because it affects future tool change costs. Enumerate candidate actions by occurrence ID then axis; test semantic preconditions before geometry. The nonnegative additive cost tuple is unrelated nonfastener removals, fastener removals, tool changes, operations. Cache geometry outcomes by revision, removed set, action, geometry policy and tool hash. A static dependency graph is a pruning aid, not a substitute for state dependent access checks.
For 12 occurrences there are at most 4096 removed sets before tool states and action alternatives; the explicit state cap therefore remains necessary. Thirty seconds or 4096 expanded states ends a search. Feasible results include their checked route even if the budget is reached later; report whether cost optimality was established within the finite supported graph. No path under supported rules requires exhaustive traversal with no unresolved eligible transitions. Any relevant unknown edge or early stop means search incomplete.
Obstruction diagnostics. Check each blocked translation against every remaining occurrence. A positive BRep intersection at a witness pose confirms that action is blocked; a conservative bounding volume overlap alone does not. A driver envelope intersecting a nonengagement obstacle confirms inaccessible modeled tool access. Insufficient clearance is a gap below the declared threshold, not necessarily a collision. A fixed bracket is an obstruction only within permitted operations; avoid saying the product is physically impossible to disassemble.
Reverse replacement. The replacement has identical declared external geometry and interfaces. Reverse the path with the same obstacles restored in reverse order and validate tool approach for reinstallation. Report geometric insertion and tool access separately. This does not verify tightening torque, electrical reconnection, calibration, consumables or restored function. If replacement shape differs, create a separate revision and reanalyze.

16 Geometry and Constraint Verification

Geometry validation flow
Verification principle. Use OCCT solids for actual geometry, and conservative collision volumes for continuous motion certification. Rendering meshes serve selection and display only. The verifier runs independently of the model controller and reconstructs candidate geometry from the recorded trusted template and parameters; it must not trust an AI supplied mesh or a candidate pass flag.
Practical continuous checking choice. For v1, represent each moving and stationary occurrence by one or more axis aligned bounding boxes that contain its solid geometry in world coordinates. The default is its complete BRep bounding box. The supported bracket constructor can emit a tighter union of boxes whose material coverage is checked. The union must conservatively cover every piece of material; it may contain extra empty space. User supplied boxes are never trusted without server validation.
For an axis aligned translation, calculate exact swept boxes and analytic interval overlap against stationary boxes. Inflate noncontact obstacles by the required gap and numerical guard. If all boxes remain separated for the whole segment, the actual solids are clear under the conservative representation. Diagonal box inflation is conservative and can reject valid narrow routes. A path cannot pass just because sampled animation frames are clear. Default part boxes cannot certify motion through a bore or access opening; such routes require a server verified tighter material decomposition. Test coverage by checking that the solid minus the proxy union contains no material above the numerical policy; ambiguous residue means unknown.
A positive proxy overlap yields a candidate collision pose for an OCCT intersection or distance query. Actual positive intersection or a measured subthreshold clearance can reject a move. If the proxies overlap but a definitive witness is absent, return unknown, optionally request a tighter trusted decomposition, and do not claim a proven obstruction or admit that action. This trades coverage for honest verification and is the primary technical risk to test on day one.
Start contacts. Ordinary gap inflation would reject every mounted part. Permit only declared, geometrically checked planar start contacts on named regions, with translation along an outward separating normal. Isolate their material volumes and verify that the two contact volumes lie in opposite closed half spaces of the named plane with no initial penetration; outward translation then maintains separation. All remaining material pairs must satisfy the ordinary swept checks. The exemption applies only to the mating region and does not establish a positive gap at time zero. If contact regions cannot be isolated without masking other material, mark the action unsupported. Model threads and drive engagement as simplified declared interaction regions, not arbitrary collision exclusions.
Geometry pipeline. Check the import solids, confirm template match, construct the revised bracket in an isolated process, validate a nonempty connected solid, compare changed and protected regions, check assembled intersections, test all hard constraints, recompute the plan from the complete original state, validate reverse insertion, and round trip export. Any failed or unknown mandatory stage prevents success. Use distance utilities to report gaps and Boolean common operations to inspect interference; check kernel completion and shape validity before interpreting their outputs. OCCT documents minimum distance and STEP assembly processing, not a universal repair validator. [S20-S21]

Lifecycle. Upload commits a quarantined package. Import creates an immutable revision and readiness report. Target selection freezes a run specification. The job executes baseline search, AI tool loop, candidate reconstruction and verification. Comparison is computed from committed reports. Acceptance writes a review event. Deployment. One Linux container image for the coordinator and geometry worker runtime, one persistent volume for SQLite and artifacts, and a built frontend served behind the same HTTPS origin. Failure and retry. Queue one job, return 202 and stream SSE stage events. Cancel changes the job state, stops new tool calls, and terminates the isolated worker at its resource boundary. Pure reads may retry; mutations use idempotency keys. Restarted workers mark unfinished jobs interrupted. Artifact writes use temporary paths and atomic rename followed by database commit; orphan cleanup never deletes the original revision.

Frozen cases. Before tuning, create 18 cases: six variants per fixture. Predeclare 12 supported positive or already accessible cases and six negative or out of scope cases: fixed blocker, clearance violation, inaccessible driver, missing relation, boundary contact and all templates disallowed. Hold out at least one geometry and one semantic constraint variant per fixture. Report results for every assigned case. Variants from a shared constructor are correlated and cannot establish broad industrial generalization.
Comparators. C0 original design with deterministic search; C1 engineer authored legal template and parameters with the same verifier; C2 rule selector that enumerates eligible template parameter grids in stable order under the same candidate and time budgets; C3 Muse proposal and feedback loop. Use a small exhaustive legal candidate search offline where feasible to establish whether any supported improvement exists. C1 is an upper reference for selection, not proof of production desirability.
For AI comparison, run each condition on all frozen cases and repeat C3 three times with recorded model version and settings. Compare successful verified improvements, mandatory violations caught, candidate calls, total latency, tool calls, and explanation evidence accuracy. A model cannot outperform complete deterministic enumeration on attainable correctness; its plausible value is fewer evaluations, better choice under a constrained budget, better handling of semantically different constraints, or clearer grounded explanation. If all cases have one obvious intervention, the benchmark cannot establish meaningful model advantage.
Model benefit gate. Predeclare a practical target of at least two additional successful held out cases under the same three candidate budget, or at least 25 percent fewer candidate checks with no loss of success. These are decision thresholds, not measured outcomes or statistically established superiority. If unmet, remove the efficiency claim, retain the rule based baseline, and reassess the AI thesis. Small sample results support a prototype decision only.

25 Long Term Product Roadmap
After the event. Validate an authorized real design review; improve metadata authoring and capture real omitted operations. Prioritize a native CAD integration only after determining whether export and reimport friction blocks adoption. Extend template coverage from actual customer access failures, with an independent regression case for every new operation.
Next technical stage. Add rotations and improved motion validation, full screwdriver geometry and additional contact families, then cables and connector handling if a customer need justifies the complexity. Structural, thermal, sealing, manufacturing and regulatory checks require qualified tools, boundary conditions and domain review. They cannot be inferred from adding a more capable language model.
Potential platform. Support design for repairability reviews across multiple priority components, versions, and repair scenarios; integrate with PDM or PLM change records and service information. Whole product scoring needs an explicit validated methodology incorporating parts, documentation, reassembly and software dependencies. It is not the sum of the prototype's action counts.
26 Business and Commercialization Hypotheses
Initial buyer. An engineering manager or head of product development with authority over design review tooling. End user. The mechanical engineer. A service specialist supplies failure priorities and challenges assumptions. Design consultancies could be early partners because they see multiple projects, but their willingness to share client CAD is uncertain.
Value hypothesis. Reduce the manual coordination needed to find a service access issue, inspect a supported change, and document the evidence before design freeze. Establish this by observing comparable reviews with and without UNLOCK, measuring active engineer time, missing constraints, edit usefulness and rework. Do not equate fewer modeled operations with reduced warranty cost or environmental impact.
Pricing experiments. Offer a tightly scoped paid design review pilot at a hypothetical $500 to $2,000 per module, or test a hypothetical $100 to $300 per active engineer monthly plan after repeated use is demonstrated. These are willingness to pay experiment anchors, not competitor prices or revenue projections. Avoid usage based pricing until runtime predictability and repeat review frequency are understood.
Acquisition. Recruit a small design consultancy, appliance service engineering team and repair focused manufacturer through authorized introductions; publish a rights cleared case study once a real review exists. No outreach is part of this PRD task. A CAD plug in may reduce workflow friction but creates version, API and procurement dependencies. Enterprise deployment may require on premise geometry processing, provider data controls, SSO, audit retention and contract review beyond the MVP.
Defensibility hypothesis. A growing corpus of permissioned failure cases, validated templates, accurate constraint provenance, integration into review decisions and transparent evidence could create durable value. Access to a general model, a STEP viewer or an isolated planner is unlikely to be sufficient. Patent review remains necessary; the public research weakens a broad algorithm moat. No market size or revenue forecast is supported.

28 Go and No Go Decision Criteria
Prototype go. By October 28, import a genuine separate part assembly, prove the continuous checker on thin obstacle and contact fixtures, produce baseline evidence and complete a real Muse tool call. 30 Final Product Recommendation
Build UNLOCK as a supported repairability redesign review tool. Start with a fan or service cartridge behind a known parametric bracket, using a complete STEP plus manifest package. Demonstrate a real edit, independent geometric revalidation, an inspectable measured comparison, and editable output. The most important early decision is whether the conservative checker can certify a useful path while correctly rejecting thin obstacles and contact errors.
Judge review. The model contribution is testable through tool logs, constraint changes and a matched rule baseline. Broad novelty is not supported. A successful film must show an actual working edit and checks, with synthetic and replay labels where applicable. The unresolved weakness is that a small template domain may give the model little advantage.
Mechanical engineering review. Explicit connections, units, contact handling, protected regions and full motion checking make the claims inspectable. Conservative proxies may reject valid paths, and collision freedom does not prove strength, electrical safety, manufacturing feasibility or restored function. The export must carry these boundaries with the design.
Product review. The engineer and decision are specific, but review frequency, semantic authoring effort and willingness to pay remain unknown. Validate one real review and a buyer action before expanding into a platform. Treat a strong prototype result as permission to learn from customers, not evidence that the commercial opportunity is established.
Proceed while the core gates hold. Preserve the central promise: select a difficult service component, change a constrained design feature, and independently verify the supported geometric improvement.
